Welcome to Noodle Empire!

Your journey starts with a tiny noodle stand and 50 coins. Take customer orders, cook the right recipe, serve before their patience runs out and reinvest your profits. Unlock new recipes, hire staff and explore new worlds.

How to play: Press Cook order, wait for the bowl to finish, then serve it. Keep customers happy to build combos and earn tips. Music starts after your first click.
